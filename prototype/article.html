Go is an abstract strategy board game for two players. It was invented in China more than 2,500 years ago and is believed to be the oldest board game continuously played to the present day. Selected events in the history of the game.

The Tokugawa shogunate patronises the game and forms the houses of professional players; Honinbo Sansa is appointed head. Huang Longshi (c. 1650 – c. 1694), one of the strongest players in history, is born. Shusaku's move 127 stuns Gennan Inseki, and becomes one of the best moves of all time. DeepMind's AlphaGo wins four games to one against Lee Sedol in Seoul.

AlphaGo wins four games to one in Seoul. AlphaGo beats the world number one, Ke Jie, three games to none. A version trained without human games is described in Nature.
